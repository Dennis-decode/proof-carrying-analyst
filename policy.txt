BUSINESS RULES
1. Revenue means orders with status 'completed' only. Cancelled orders are not revenue.
2. Gross revenue = sum of completed orders in USD. Net revenue = gross revenue minus VALID refunds.
3. A refund is valid only if its order exists, the order is completed, and the refund is not larger than the order.
4. All money is reported in USD. EUR orders are converted with the monthly rate in rates.csv.
5. Quarter 1 means January, February and March 2025.
